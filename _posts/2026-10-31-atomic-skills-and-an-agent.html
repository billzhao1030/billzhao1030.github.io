Atomic Skills, One Grip, and an Agent

Xunyi Zhao · ~2 min read · project page

Two robot hands pick up a scrambled Rubik's cube and solve it — the way a person does. No end-to-end policy, no 20-move optimal solver: twelve learned moves that always come home to the same grip, and an agent that plans with CFOP. Nine random scrambles, nine solves.
